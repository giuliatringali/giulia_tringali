I'm a PhD Student in Peace Economics at [Sapienza University of Rome](https://phd.uniroma1.it/web/NATIONAL-PHD-IN-PEACE-STUDIES_nD4085_IT.aspx) and Research Fellow at the [International Peace Science Center (IPSC)](https://centridiricerca.unicatt.it/ipsc/en.html) in Milan. My PhD work is on state capacity and peacebuilding with a focus on Sub-Saharian Africa. I'm currently working with quantitative methods on climate-conflict nexus looking at food prices, and the relation between public finance and political violence. 

My research interests grows from field experience and seeks to connect ground-level realities to structural explainantios of conflict and fragility. Before, I've worked as a project manager in humanitarian settings across West Africa (Burkina Faso, Benin, Senegal) in particular with youth and women on food security, rural development and agroecology.

I'm also passionate about journalism and I'm also working as a freelance in since 2019 and I'm member of [FADA collective](https://www.instagram.com/fadacollective/) and [FuoriFuoco](https://www.fuorifuococomo.it/).
